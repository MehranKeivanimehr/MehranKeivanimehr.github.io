About

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Florida, where I work with Dr. Sanjeev Koppal. My research is on the energy efficiency of AI systems: I build analytical and measurement-based models of where energy goes in deep learning, especially large-kernel networks on modern GPUs and on optical computing hardware, and use them to design more energy-efficient learning systems.

Recent projects include Multi-GPU Systems Lab, a study of what makes multi-GPU training and LLM serving benchmarks trustworthy; NCCL Energy Frontier, a study of whether the fastest GPU collective-communication settings are also the most energy-efficient; a measurement study of PyTorch DDP training with a straggling GPU; LocalClinical-Tuner, a fine-tuned 7B clinical language model that runs fully offline on local hardware; Patients, Not Slices, a leakage-controlled brain-tumor detection study on MRI with patient-level conformal guarantees; and ThermalVLA, a thermal-aware navigation policy for robots on a Jetson (in progress). I have also built backpropagation-free binary CNNs for an event-camera-based opto-electronic convolution accelerator and a framework that shows where large-kernel networks become memory-bound.

I received my M.Sc. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Florida in 2025 and my B.Sc. from the University of Kashan, Iran.
